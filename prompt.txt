Topics to EXCLUDE:
	•	LHC experimental results (ATLAS, CMS, LHCb, ALICE), unless they provide directly transferable techniques for non-LHC experiments
	•	Pure theory or formal phenomenology without direct experimental validation
	•	Phenomenology papers not tied to real data or near-term measurements
	•	Detector or accelerator upgrades with >5-year deployment horizon
	•	Heavy-ion physics
	•	Cosmology papers without particle-physics experimental input
	•	Duplicate submissions or proceedings without substantial new material
	•	Pure software/framework papers without demonstrated physics or detector impact

Topics to INCLUDE:
	•	Neutrino physics:
	    Oscillation measurements, cross sections, nuclear effects
	    Sterile neutrino searches
	    Results from SBN, T2K, NOvA, MicroBooNE, DUNE prototypes, JUNO, Hyper-K, etc.
        Neutrino mass and mixing measurements
	•	Dark matter searches:
	    Direct detection, accelerator-based (non-LHC), and neutrino-based searches
	    New limits, signals, or experimental techniques
	•	Detector R&D with near-term application (≤5 years), especially:
	    LArTPCs, photon detection, cryogenic electronics, DAQ
	    Calibration, reconstruction, and simulation improvements
	•	Advanced analysis methods (ML/AI, novel reconstruction or inference techniques) demonstrated on real data or high-fidelity simulation, with clear experimental relevance
	•	Results or methods that reduce systematics, improve sensitivity, or inform experimental design/strategy

Output Format:
- If the paper is irrelevant based on the topics above, output the single word: IRRELEVANT
- If the paper is relevant, provide a 2-sentence summary.
